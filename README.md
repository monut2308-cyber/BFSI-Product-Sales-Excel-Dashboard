# 📊 BFSI Financial Products Sales & Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end **Excel Business Intelligence & Sales Analytics Dashboard** analyzing 1,000 financial product applications across 8 Indian cities and 4 distribution channels (January 2026 – June 2026). The goal is to evaluate product revenue share, channel conversion efficiency, customer demographics, and sales executive performance.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
- **Power Query (ETL):** Deduplicated records (`1,002 → 1,000` unique applications), standardized inconsistent text casing (`Product` & `Application_Status`), handled missing values (`Unknown` / `0`), and engineered derived features (`Commission_Earned_INR`, `Month_Name`, `Day_Name`, `Age_Group`).
- **Power Pivot & Data Modeling:** Structured transactional and summary tables for multi-dimensional aggregation.
- **Pivot Tables & Charts:** Built dynamic visualizations with chronological sorting and custom number formatting (`₹`).
- **Interactive Controls:** Connected `Application_Date` Timeline and Slicers (`Customer_Segment`, `Application_Status`, `Product`) across all visuals.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
1. **Top Revenue Driver:** **Personal Loans** generated the highest total revenue (`₹22.86L`), followed by Credit Cards (`₹8.82L`).
2. **High-Value Segment:** **Salaried** customers recorded the highest average ticket size across all customer segments.
3. **Demographic Trend:** The **18–30 age bracket** achieved the highest application approval/success rate.
4. **Top Markets:** **Gurugram, Noida, Lucknow, Delhi, and Ghaziabad** lead in total application volume.
5. **Channel Performance:** **Telecalling** delivered the highest conversion rate, while **Branch** experienced the highest cancellation rate.
6. **Sales Team Ranking:** **EX003, EX011, and EX013** are the top 3 executives by revenue and volume, whereas **EX004, EX006, and EX001** ranked lowest.
7. **Seasonality:** **June** was the peak month (`₹8.55L` revenue), and **Thursday** recorded the highest weekly submission volume.
